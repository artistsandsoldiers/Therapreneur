# Claims go to medical aids by email, not a switching house

For v1, claims are emailed as PDF invoices in a claim batch to each medical aid's claims address, and the medical aid replies with a reference number. Switching houses (MediKredit, SwitchOn) would give real-time responses, but their terms aren't public and they add cost per claim and an integration we can't justify before validation. This mirrors what practitioners already do by hand. Revisit once paying practices ask for faster responses.
