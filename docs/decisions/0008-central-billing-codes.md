# We keep billing codes and medical aid rates centrally

Billing codes and each medical aid's rates are one list we maintain for all practices, tagged by profession, instead of each practice typing in its own. A practitioner picks a price adjustment (the medical aid rate, or a percentage above or below it), and each invoice line's price follows from it and the patient's medical aid. Practitioners find loading codes by hand a pain, and keeping them current is a selling point, but it commits us to updating rates whenever medical aids publish new ones. Rates are VAT inclusive and adjusted prices round to the nearest cent. Until we hold other medical aids' rates, every patient, patients who pay themselves included, is priced from the Discovery rate.
