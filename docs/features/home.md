# Home

**Status:** building

## Problem
Practitioners have to open several screens to see how the practice is doing and what needs doing.

## Evidence
None yet. Barry's idea (28 Oct 2026), to test with Robs.

## Scope
- The first page after logging in, and where the practice name, logo and "Home" in the menu go.
- A greeting by South African time with the practitioner's first name, and a New session button.
- A bento grid of live tiles, each opening its screen: income this month (with six months of bars), tax, owed to you, claims to send, waiting on medical aids, invoices, patients and expenses.
- A teal dot on tiles that need doing: claims to send, or tax due within 30 days.
- Phones get one column, the tiles that need doing first.

## Out of scope
- A to-do strip of reminders (yearly tax check, missing notes). The page leaves room for it above the tiles.
- A referrers tile, until referrers are built.
