# Expenses

**Status:** building

## Problem
Practitioners know nothing about accounting or tax. Their expenses and receipts sit in a sparse spreadsheet and a shoebox, so tax time is painful and they can't see their profit.

## Evidence
Robynne Stock (solo adult OT) tracks expenses in Excel, October 2026; Barry says her spreadsheet is very sparse. First piece of the [tax pack plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/L9D4kUiUgPbVBYn39rpAhF). Grilled with Barry on 28 October 2026.

## Scope
- Expenses in the main nav: newest first, grouped by month with totals, the tax year so far (from 1 March), and expenses missing a receipt.
- Add an expense on the phone: amount, date paid, who you paid, category, optional note, optional receipt.
- A receipt is a photo or PDF up to 10 MB, kept privately until the expense is deleted. Big photos shrink before upload.
- Categories come from a central list we keep, each with a hint of what goes in it. A practitioner can add her own while adding an expense and rename it in Settings. Categories are never deleted.
- Edit or delete any expense at any time.
- Performance shows expenses and profit before tax (invoiced income less expenses) per month and for the tax year so far, with expenses by category.

## Out of scope
- Bank statement import, recurring expenses, VAT.
- Mapping categories to SARS and the tax year summary. They are the next pieces of the tax pack. Vehicle costs count their logbook business share (travel.md).
- A screen for us to manage the central category list. An agent changes it for now.
