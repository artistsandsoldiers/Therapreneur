# Reconciliation

**Status:** grilled

## Problem
Working out which claims a medical aid has paid means comparing its remittance against invoices by hand.

## Evidence
Discovery interview with Robynne Stock (solo adult OT), October 2026. Medical aids publish remittances on their portals on their own schedule.

## Scope
- Bring in a medical aid's remittance.
- Match each line to its claim by member number, date and amount, and confirm the matches in one step.
- Matched invoices become paid, with the payment date.

## Out of scope
- Shortfalls, rejections and part-payments, for now. We will build for them later, so don't design them out.

## Open questions
- How remittances arrive in practice (PDF, CSV, portal export) for each medical aid.
