# Notes

**Status:** grilled

## Problem
Session notes live apart from billing, and it's easy to fall behind on them.

## Evidence
Discovery interview with Robynne Stock (solo adult OT), October 2026. Notes in the same tool were on her wishlist.

## Scope
- One free-text note per session, captured with it or added later.
- A patient's notes read as a timeline on their page.
- A notes review lists every past session without a note.

## Out of scope
- SOAP or other templates.
- Blocking invoices on a missing note (see decision 0003).

## Open questions
- Record-keeping and POPIA requirements before real patient data is stored.
