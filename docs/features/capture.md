# Capture

**Status:** grilled

## Problem
Practitioners bill from memory at the end of the day or week, which is slow and leaks revenue.

## Evidence
Discovery interview with Robynne Stock (solo adult OT), October 2026. She sees 3 to 6 patients a day and invoices each session separately.

## Scope
- A phone screen that opens on the session that just ended, with the patient's ICD-10 codes filled in.
- Pick one or more billing codes, with prices from the price list. One tap for cancelled or no-show.
- Type or dictate an optional note.
- Saving creates the session's invoice.

## Out of scope
- Booking or editing sessions (see decision 0002).

## Open questions
- Real billing codes and prices. The prototype's are placeholders.
