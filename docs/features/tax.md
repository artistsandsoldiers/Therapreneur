# Tax

**Status:** building

## Problem
Provisional tax is one of the most stressful parts of a solo practitioner's year. She has to guess the year's tax twice a year, and a low guess costs a 20% penalty plus interest.

## Evidence
Robynne Stock asked for it and works it out crudely in her spreadsheet, October 2026. Grilled with Barry on 28 October 2026. [Design and SARS rules](https://claude.ai/artifact/2qmuRjpMt8P562q9hG6gvT).

## Scope
- Tax in the main nav. Off until the practitioner answers a few questions, one per screen, and she can turn it off.
- Questions: date of birth, rough income and spending from 1 March until her records here start, medical aid members, monthly retirement annuity, other income (salary and PAYE, interest, rent, other), any payment already made, and optionally last year's taxable income. Answers carry over to the next tax year.
- The headline: what to put aside each month and roughly what to pay by the next due date (31 August, end of February, or the 30 September top-up).
- The working, line by line: profit so far stretched to a full year in a straight line (or her own figure), deductions, SARS's tables, rebates, medical aid credit, PAYE and payments made.
- A penalty-safe total from last year's taxable income, up to R1 million.
- Payments to SARS, recorded by hand.
- An estimate, not advice: every expense counts in full, and nothing is sent to SARS.
- Sole proprietors only. Settings says whether a practice is an Inc, and an Inc sees no estimate.

## Out of scope
- Reminders before due dates and a yearly "has anything changed?" check. They belong to a future home and reminders page.
- Travel and the vehicle logbook, wear and tear, the extra medical expenses credit, capital gains, donations.
- Filing with SARS, and company tax for Incs.
