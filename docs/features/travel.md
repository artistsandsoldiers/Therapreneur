# Travel logbook

**Status:** building

## Problem
Practitioners who drive to patients can claim the business share of their car costs, but only with a SARS logbook of every business trip. Keeping one is so much admin that many claim nothing.

## Evidence
Robynne Stock (solo adult OT) does home visits and uses sessional rooms, and claims no travel today because of the admin, October 2026. Grilled with Barry on 30 October 2026 ([plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/TEs2GcE1tPbZdoYo1oxpjD)).

## Scope
- Settings: home address (work days start and end there), places where she sees patients, and the car used for work with odometer readings. Changing car ends the old one with its last reading.
- Each patient is usually seen at one of her places or at home, with their home address for home visits.
- The calendar's "New" adds a patient session, meeting, training or errand. Non-session entries have a place or address and make trips, never invoices.
- Trips are worked out automatically from the calendar, with no confirm step: home, each stop in time order, home. Home to a regular place (anywhere she sees patients) is private, as SARS says. Everything else is business, except home to a patient's home while the cautious setting is on.
- Distances are typed once per pair of addresses and remembered. Google Maps comes later.
- A Logbook screen per car and tax year, with the business share from odometer readings, exported in SARS's columns with patient names or anonymised.
- Vehicle costs count only their business share on Performance and Tax. Parking and tolls on business trips count in full.

## Out of scope
- GPS tracking, wear and tear, more than one car at a time, location types like hospitals.
- Travel fees on invoices: practitioners add the billing code themselves.
