# Claims

**Status:** grilled

## Problem
Emailing invoices to each medical aid and tracking which ones were received is manual and easy to lose track of.

## Evidence
Discovery interview with Robynne Stock (solo adult OT), October 2026. She emails PDFs to per-scheme addresses and gets a reference number back.

## Scope
- Group ready claims by medical aid, so one send makes one claim batch per medical aid.
- A templated email listing patient, member number and ICD-10 codes, with PDFs attached.
- Preview before sending.
- Record the reference number against every claim in the batch.
- Cash invoices are emailed to the patient instead.

## Out of scope
- Switching houses (see decision 0001).

## Open questions
- Each medical aid's real claims address and email format.
