# Reports

**Status:** grilled

## Problem
Practitioners need a separate tool to see how the practice is doing.

## Evidence
Discovery interview with Robynne Stock (solo adult OT), October 2026. She uses Tiny Invoice for these reports today.

## Scope
- Income per month: invoiced and paid, last 6 months.
- Income per patient: invoiced and paid, with payer type.
- Outstanding: unpaid invoices by age (0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, 90+ days), by medical aid and cash.

## Out of scope
- Expenses and tax.

- None.
