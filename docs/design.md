# Design

_Limit: 300 words._

**Modern, minimal, sleek, professional.** Think Notion: white space, quiet chrome, and content first.

## Look

- White background, greys for structure, and one accent colour: teal `#0E7C86`. No purple. The accent marks the primary action and little else.
- One sans-serif typeface (Inter). Hierarchy comes from size and weight, not colour.
- Hairline borders over shadows and boxes. Generous spacing.
- The app wears the practice's logo and name in its menu, so it feels like their own. Therapreneur stays in the background. The logo also goes on invoices and claims.

## Screens

- **One main nav.** A left sidebar on desktop; on a phone, a menu button opens it from the right. Every top-level page goes in it.
- **Capture is phone-first.** It's used straight after a session, one-handed, in under a minute.
- **Admin is desktop-first** (claims, reconciliation, performance, settings) but must still work on a phone.
- **Desktop uses the full width** beside the sidebar, capped only on huge screens. Lists become tables with column headers, dashboards sit side by side, and forms keep a comfortable width. Phones get the same content as two-line rows.

## Tone of voice

- Plain, warm and brief. Short labels, no jargon.
- South African English: rands (R1,250.00), "medical aid", DD/MM/YYYY dates on invoices and PDFs. On screen, dates read "Today, Wed 28 Oct" or "3 days ago, Sun 25 Oct" within a week, else "Mon 12 Oct".
