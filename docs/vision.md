# Vision

_Limit: 250 words._

## Why

Allied health practitioners train to treat patients, not to run a business. In private practice they lose hours to invoicing, medical aid claims and chasing payments across tools that don't talk to each other, and they make business and financial decisions with little support.

## What

A combination of software, services and business coaching that helps them run the business side of their practice more efficiently and effectively, and make better business and financial decisions.

## Who

- **Now:** solo practitioners in South Africa: occupational therapists, physiotherapists and speech therapists.
- **Later:** larger practices.

## 12-month goal

Roughly 200 practices paying around R500 a month. Pricing is a guess until we've done market research.

## Open questions

- How software, services and coaching fit together and which leads. This depends on market research.
- What we will deliberately never be.
