# Changelog

Changes a practitioner would notice, newest first. Each entry is one line, and the PR link holds the detail.

## Unreleased

- Logbook: your business trips for SARS, worked out from your calendar with no admin. Type each distance once, add an odometer reading now and then, and see your business km and business share per car and tax year. Download it as a spreadsheet, with or without patient names.
- Calendar: "New" now adds a patient session, meeting, training, errand or other work. Sessions say where they are (one of your places or the patient's home), starting where the patient is usually seen, and you can change it. Meetings and errands take a place or an address, for your travel logbook.
- Travel (first step of the logbook): Settings now takes your home address, the places you see patients, and your car with odometer readings, including changing car. Each patient can be usually seen at one of your places or at their home, with their address.
- Calendar: book sessions ahead on a week view (one day at a time on a phone), repeat them weekly or every 2 weeks, drag to move or change length on desktop, and delete them. Once a booked session is over, capture it or mark a no-show in one tap. Home shows how many are waiting to be captured.
- Home: the first page after logging in greets you by name and shows live tiles for income, tax, what you're owed, claims, invoices, patients and expenses, each opening its screen. Setup and Settings now ask for your first and last name.
- Referrers: record who referred each patient (a doctor, your website, social media and so on), add new ones from the patient form, and see and filter income by referrer on Performance.
- Tax: a timeline at the top shows the year's payment dates, what you've paid and what's due, and where today is.
- Tax: turn on a provisional tax estimate from your invoices and expenses, see what to put aside each month and pay by the next SARS date, and record payments. Settings now asks whether your practice is a sole proprietor or an Inc.
- Dates on screen read like "Yesterday, Tue 27 Oct". Invoices, patients, expenses and sent claims have filters. Pricing in Settings is a slider around medical aid rates. The logo has a pencil to change it. Performance has Overall, Income and Expenses tabs for any month, tax year or dates.
- New session and Add patient open as a pop-up. Sessions have a length (your usual one is in Settings), so they show an end time, also on the invoice PDF. Billing codes you used before for a patient show as chips, and "Same as last time" copies their last invoice's codes.
- Patients: whether a patient has medical aid is now separate from who pays you, and each session can change who pays. Invoices a patient pays still show their medical aid details so they can claim back.
- Desktop uses the whole screen: lists show as tables with columns, Performance sits side by side, and an invoice sits beside its payment and session details. Phones look the same as before.
- Expenses: add what you spend with a receipt photo or PDF, in plain categories you can add to, and see profit before tax on Performance.
- Reconcile: tick the claims a medical aid's remittance paid to mark them paid on its date, see each remittance's total, and undo one recorded by mistake.
- Practice logo: upload it in Settings and it shows in the menu, on invoices and on claim PDFs.
- Performance: a live dashboard of income per month, income per patient, and what's outstanding by age and payer. Mark any invoice paid, with a date.
- Claims: email medical aid invoices to each medical aid in one batch with PDFs attached, record the reference number, and download any invoice as a PDF. Patients get date of birth, dependant code and referring doctor; Settings gets the HPCSA number.
- A Patients page: search the list, edit details, see each patient's sessions and notes, move them to past patients, and a new menu (sidebar on desktop, pop-up on a phone).
- Practitioners log in with their email and a password, set up their practice and prices, and capture a session on their phone to create its invoice.
- Landing page with a waiting list signup (name, email, occupation).
- Project context docs for coding agents: vision, features, glossary, design and decisions.
